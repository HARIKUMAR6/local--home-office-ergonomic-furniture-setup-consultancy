Ergonomic seating

Choosing a chair that fits

Four things worth checking before you decide.

Adjustability and fit
Seat height and depth, back support and armrests that can be set to suit you and your desk.
Desk and chair compatibility
You should be able to sit with your feet supported and your forearms comfortably at desk height.
Armrests and back support
Armrests that tuck under the desk when needed, and a backrest that supports the length of your back.
Space and daily work
Room to roll and turn, the hours you sit, whether the chair is shared and what floor it sits on.

This is general guidance. Comfort is personal and it isn't medical advice.

Desks and screen setup

Set the work surface, then the screen

  • Height-adjustable desks

    Measure the width and depth you can spare, and the clearance the desk needs to move up and down.

  • Monitor arms

    Check how your desk edge will take a clamp or fixing, and that the arm suits your screen's size and weight.

  • Laptop stands

    Raise the laptop so the screen sits nearer eye level, and pair it with a separate keyboard and mouse.

  • Screen risers

    A simple lift for monitors on desks that can't take an arm, with space left underneath.
