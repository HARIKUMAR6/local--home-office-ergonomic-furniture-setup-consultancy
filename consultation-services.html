What the consultation covers

Four things we look at in your space

A

Space and room dimensions

We start with the room: its size, the floor space you can use and where doors, windows and sockets sit.

B

Suitable furniture categories

From that we identify which kinds of furniture suit the space, such as a compact desk or a chair that tucks away.

C

Desk and screen positioning

We review how desk, chair and screen relate: screen height and distance, and where keyboard and mouse sit.

D

Lighting, organisation and daily needs

Lighting, storage and the way you work shape what to keep, add or leave out.

Workspace assessment

What helps us assess your workspace

A few details are enough to begin. Share what you can.

  • Room size and floor space

    Rough measurements of the room and the area you can use.

  • Your current desk, chair and equipment

    What you already have and what is and isn't working.

  • Daily routine and requirements

    How long you work, what you work on and who else uses the space.

  • Budget or priorities (optional)

    Only if you'd like us to take them into account.

You don't need to share anything sensitive. Please leave out personal or financial account details.

Furniture recommendations

From your space to a shortlist

Once we understand how you work, recommendations follow from the space rather than a standard list.

Seating and desk categories

Chairs and desks matched to the room, how long you sit and the height you work at.

Monitor and laptop positioning

Arms, stands or risers that bring the screen to a comfortable height and distance.

Useful accessories

Keyboard and mouse supports, footrests and the small additions that make a desk easier to use.

Organisation and lighting

Space-conscious storage and task lighting that keep the desk clear and the screen glare-free.
